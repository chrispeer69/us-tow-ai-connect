# The ecosystem knowledge pack

What the inbound agent is allowed to say about the twenty-eight solutions in the
US Tow Alliance ecosystem, and — more importantly — what it is **not**.

Source of truth for the tile list is `ECOSYSTEM_SERVICES` in the Alliance repo
(`towing/views.py`), which is what renders the grid on the landing page and on
`/services`. Every fact below was read off the live public site on
**2026-08-22**. Nothing here is inferred from a tile description.

Re-verify before quoting any figure to a caller. Five of these sites changed
status between the 2026-08-21 pass and this one.

---

## Why this document exists

The agent has invented policy on live calls before. On a line where the people
answering run small businesses on thin margins, a number guessed wrong is a
number they will plan around. So the pack sorts every solution into one of
three buckets, and the bucket decides what the agent may say:

| Bucket | What the agent may say |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | The description AND the published prices |
| **Named only** | What it is, one line. No numbers, no features |
| **Do not describe** | That it is not up yet, and nothing else |

A tile existing on the website is not evidence that the product exists.

## Verified — describe freely, prices are real

### US Tow Alliance — ustowalliance.com
The national registry and the umbrella over everything else. A basic profile is
free. This is what the outbound campaign is calling about.

**Do not quote member, city or carrier counts.** They are live counters and they
disagree across our own properties: the Alliance homepage showed 12.0K direct
lines / 4,350 cities / "52 + 1 States" on 2026-08-22, US Tow Marketing showed
10.6K carriers / 3,588 cities / 50 states the same day, and the previous version
of this pack said 18,300 / 5,283. Say "thousands of operators, all fifty
states."

### US Tow AI Connect — ustowaiconnect.com
The 24/7 AI phone dispatcher. Answers inbound, gives live ETAs out of their
dispatch software, handles impound questions, routes day/night, speaks Spanish,
and makes the outbound confirmation calls that carry the repair-shop referral.

- Alliance Profile — **$0.30/min, $499 one-time setup**
- Alliance Elite — **$0.25/min, $399 setup**
- AI-Connect Shareholder — **$0.20/min, setup waived**
- **14-day free trial, no credit card**

It is a free TRIAL, not a free tier. Never say "there's a free version" — they
find out on day fifteen.

### US Tow Fleet — ustowfleet.com
Fractional fleet management for small operators: DOT compliance, driver safety,
maintenance coordination, insurance management. Ohio-focused. Roughly 3–10
trucks is the target.

- **$149/month per service** (pick 1–4)
- **$399/month all-access** (all four)
- No long-term contract, cancel anytime, no payment to apply
- The site claims "Average $8K+ insurance savings" — attribute it as *their
  claim*, never as a promise

### US Tow Jobs — ustowjobs.com (301s to towtruckjobs.online)
Hiring board for towing and box-truck companies. Flat rate, not per-click.

- Drivers — **$9.95/month**
- Companies — **$349/month, unlimited listings**
- Enterprise — custom

### US Auto Repair Jobs — usautorepairjobs.com
The same idea for repair and body shops. "Vault" technician profiles, matching,
direct messaging.

- Technician Solo — **$9.95/month**
- Employer Starter — **$99/month**
- Employer Pro — **$249/month**
- Enterprise — custom

### US Tow Bid — ustowbid.com
Tow-truck-only marketplace: live auctions and fixed-price listings.

- Single listing — **$99 one-time**
- Multi-listing — **$799/year, up to 10**
- Enterprise — **$4,999/year, unlimited**

Do **not** quote the activity counters ("247 active listings", "$2.4M volume",
"1,892 registered buyers"). Same reason as the Alliance counts.

### US Tow Marketing — ustowmarketing.com
AI-powered 24/7 lead generation, sales promotion and brand development, built on
GHL. Leads are qualified overnight and funnelled into one platform for review
each morning. DIY or fully managed.

- Shareholder — **$199/month**
- Elite Profile Member — **$299/month**
- Profile Member — **$399/month**
- **12-month minimum agreement**
- **No free trial** — the entry point is a free weekly Zoom

Note the inversion: Shareholder is the *cheapest* tier here, unlike everywhere
else. Read the tier name back before quoting a price.

### US Tow Shield / CLAIM SHIELD — ustowshield.com
Damage-claim management: evidence aggregation from the tow (photos, walkaround
video, BOL signature, GPS timestamps), a guided defence workflow, a motor-club
rep directory, and one-click expert review by Chris Peer and the Expert Network.

**Free to Alliance members.** This is a fourth free-with-membership item and the
tile data does not flag it.

Do **not** quote the dashboard figures — "82% dispute win rate", "$1.2M
reversed", "3+ alliance operators", "147 expert reviews", "$0 average
negotiation savings". They are inconsistent with each other and read as seeded
data. The "$5,000 to $30,000 a year" range and the "$8,400 / $12,200" averages
are the site's framing of the industry problem, not a promise about their yard.

### US Tow Credit / CreditReclaim — ustowcredit.com
**Status changed.** This was serving the unconfigured "Create Next App" default
page on 2026-08-21. It is now a live credit-repair platform — education modules
and dispute-letter templates.

- DIY — **$149/month**
- Managed (they send certified mail and track disputes) — **$249/month**

It brands itself **CreditReclaim**, not US Tow Credit. If a caller goes looking
for "US Tow Credit" they will land on a different name; say so.

### PEER Field Press — peerfieldpress.com
Chris Peer's towing library: an eight-volume Core Library plus a Deep Dive
series, ten volumes in total.

- Complete 10-volume library — **$199** (site says "save $50")
- Branded re-skin of Vol. 05 (The Handbook) under the customer's own brand and
  logo, uploaded to KDP under their title — **$199 one-time**

**The free-with-membership item is one volume, not the library.** The site is
specific: activating a new US Tow Alliance profile gets you **Vol. 07 · The
Standard**, the leadership field guide, free — a stated $24.99 value. The tile
says "10-book towing leadership library" and it is free-flagged, which reads as
though the whole set is included. It is not. Never promise the library.

### Capital Bridge — capitalbridge.vip
Financing brokerage: commercial truck loans, equipment finance, MCA, lines of
credit, through a network of lenders.

- Commercial truck loans — **$15K–$1M+**
- Equipment financing — **up to $5M, fixed rate, 2–7 year terms**
- Merchant cash advance — **$5K–$500K, 24hr funding**
- Line of credit — **$10K–$250K revolving**
- **Free to apply, no hard credit pull**

Funding "in as little as 24 hours" is their wording. Do not restate it as what
the caller will get.

### RECON AI — reconai.manus.space
AI-assisted QuickBooks catch-up reconciliation, flat per-account per-month,
white-label available, 2–3 week turnaround.

**It is sold to CPAs and EAs, not to towing companies.** The site says outright
it does not market to small businesses directly. If a tower asks, that is the
honest answer — it is for their accountant.

### RECON AI DIY Toolkit — reconaitool.manus.space
A **free** monthly bank-reconciliation workbook for QuickBooks: an 8-tab
workbook, a paper checklist, and a reusable AI prompt.

## Named only — say what it is, no numbers, no features

### US Tow Dispatch — ustowdispatch.com
The dispatch software. AI-powered, cloud storage, data export, driver
coordination by company code and in-cab tablet. **Free and unlimited for Elite
members** — and that condition never comes off the sentence. The site publishes
no prices at all, only "membership provides pricing benefits". So: never quote a
dispatch price, and never say "free" without "for Elite members".

### US Tow AI — ustowai.com
The industry's dedicated AI, trained on the work across five trades — towing,
recovery, repossession, auto repair, auto body — with a credibility score on
every answer. No pricing published.

### US Tow Grade / TowGrade — ustowgrade.com
Verified, anonymous grading of roadside providers by the operators who work for
them, across 12 operational categories. **Free with membership.** No pricing
published.

### Shift Track — shifttrack.online
A driver shift-report app (installable to a phone home screen). **Free with
membership.** The public page is a driver sign-in; there is nothing else to
describe, so do not try.

### US Tow News — ustownews.com
Industry news, events and video. Free to join.

### US Tow X — ustowx.com
The industry's social network. Verified business badges and an invite-only
Owners Lounge for vetted company owners.

### Blue Collar Tips — bluecollartips.app
Employees show a QR code after a job; customers rate, review and tip by card or
P2P. Tips split **80/10/10**, tracked in real time, white-labelable. No pricing
published.

### Blue Collar AI — bluecollarai.online
The AI consulting firm that builds everything in the grid. Free 30-minute
consultation, no 12-month contracts. No pricing published.

### AI Survival School — aisurvivalschool.online
Training for owners and staff on using AI in daily operations. Taught by Chris,
who runs a 16-truck towing business. No pricing published — the "$2,500/mo"
figure on the page is what Chris *used to pay* for software, not a price.

### ConViniCar / ROADSIDE Emergency Management App — convinicar.online
A CONVINI, Inc. product, and the only consumer-facing one: the vehicle owner
taps SOS and towing, repair, body shop and rental are dispatched together. Free
to register. There is a "Join Our Network" path for towing companies.

Its four sub-brands — ConviniINSURANCE, ConviniTRAVEL, ConviniREWARDS,
ConviniTICKETS — are all marked **COMING SOON**. Do not describe them.

### Roadside MC — roadsidemc.com
The industry-owned motor club. The public page is a **partner portal login** —
we do not know the terms from the site, so send them there and stop.

## Operational flags raised by this pass

Not agent instructions — things for Chris.

1. **US Tow Stats is protected by the password `1234`,** and the page says so in
   plain text to anybody who loads it. It is linked from the public ecosystem
   grid.
2. **Three public tiles point at demo or template pages** — US Tow Command
   (RouteVision), Insurance Solutions, Iron Horse. Two of them publish fake
   contact numbers, and all three publish numbers a prospect could reasonably
   read as ours.
3. **MCA Debt Solutions is dead** and still tiled.
4. **PEER Field Press is flagged `free_with_membership`** in `ECOSYSTEM_SERVICES`
   with the description "10-book towing leadership library". The site gives one
   volume free on profile activation. Either the tile or the site needs to move.
5. **The member/city/carrier counters disagree across our own properties.** One
   number, one source, or the agent has to refuse the question.
6. **US Tow Credit now serves as "CreditReclaim".** The tile name and the site
   name do not match.

## Keeping it current

```bash
cd packages/api
RETELL_API_KEY=... node scripts/usta-inbound-kb.js --dry-run   # show the diff
RETELL_API_KEY=... node scripts/usta-inbound-kb.js --apply     # publish + re-pin
```

The section it writes is delimited in the prompt by `## THE ECOSYSTEM` and the
next `##` heading, so editing this file and re-running replaces only that block.
