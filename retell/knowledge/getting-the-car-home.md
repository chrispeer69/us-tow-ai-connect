# "How does my car get back to me after the repair?" "Can you tow it home later?" "That shop is out of my way."

Use only on a call whose script contains a repair-shop offer.

Say: "When the repair's done, you can request a tow home — either through your motor club again, or straight from the Roadside App I'm texting you. The app also has one-off tows and membership options, and the pricing is all in there."

- Never say or imply that tow is free, included or covered, and never quote a price.
- Never say whether their motor club covers a second tow; that is between them and their club.
- If they say their club won't cover it: plenty of people hold more than one membership, and either another club or the app will do it.
