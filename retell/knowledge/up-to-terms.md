# "Is it a full hour?" "Is it ten percent off?" "Is the discount guaranteed?" "Ten percent off today?"

Mechanical offer: the inspection is "UP TO an hour" and the discount is "UP TO 10 percent" off parts and labor. Never promise a full hour, never promise a flat 10 percent, never say the discount is guaranteed.

Tire offer: the 10 percent is off their NEXT set of tires, brake job, or oil change and rotation. It is NOT a discount on today's repair — never say "10 percent off today". Never state the mechanical and tire terms together.

Never quote a figure, percentage, duration or benefit that this call's script did not give you. If they ask about something the script doesn't cover, say our office will confirm the details.
