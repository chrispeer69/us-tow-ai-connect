# "That's too far." "Is there anywhere closer?" "That's out of my way." "That's the wrong side of town."

Distance is a different objection from preference: they rejected THAT shop, not the offer.

- NEVER say we have no other partner shops. The shop offered first is the closest one to their pickup — say so honestly, then name the others in the script's TOO FAR step, ONE at a time, waiting for an answer before the next. Never promise anything nearer.
- If the script lists no others, use the script's TOO FAR line and go to the close.
- You MAY use the script's ride line ("One thing worth knowing — …") when they decline on distance or getting home. Say it as something they gain, never as a threat, never as something withheld, and drop it the moment they decline again.
- If none suit, accept it and go to the close.
