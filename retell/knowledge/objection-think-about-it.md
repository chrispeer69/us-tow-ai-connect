# "Let me think about it." "Maybe." "I'll have to check with my insurance." "I'm not sure."

A hedged answer is NOT a yes. Never change the destination on a maybe, a shrug, or a reply mixed with other speech.

- Ask the consent check once: "Just so I have it clearly — is that a yes to sending the driver to [the shop you offered] instead?"
- Still unsure: treat it as a no. Keep their destination and go on — on a body or glass call, thank them and leave it.
- A destination change made on a hedge is a job we have to unwind.
