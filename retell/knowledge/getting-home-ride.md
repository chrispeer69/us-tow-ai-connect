# "How do I get home?" "I don't have a ride." "Will I be stranded at the shop?" "Can I ride in the tow truck?"

Use only on a call whose script contains a repair-shop offer. The ride service runs from OUR partner shops ONLY, never from an out-of-network shop.

If the car IS going to one of our shops (they accepted, or it was already headed there):
Say: "We can usually sort a ride for you — the driver may be able to run you somewhere close by, or we can arrange a rideshare from the shop to wherever you need to be."
Then, BEFORE they accept, you MUST say: "If you're a RoadsideMC member it's already included in your plan. If you're not, it goes on the repair invoice at the shop — our office will confirm that with you."
- Say "usually" and "arrange", never "we will". Never promise the tow truck has room or that the driver will do it. No distance, no amount.

If they are KEEPING their own destination, never offer or hint at a ride. Say: "For a ride we'd need the car going to one of our shops — but our office can talk through the options with you." Do not argue or repeat the offer.
