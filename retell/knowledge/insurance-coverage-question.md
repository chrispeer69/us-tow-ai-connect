# "Does my insurance cover this?" "Do you take my warranty?" "Will my policy pay for it?"

Use only on a call whose script contains a repair-shop offer.

Say: "Our partner shops take most aftermarket repair policies. I can note down who you're insured with and have our office team check your coverage with them directly. The diagnostic itself is free either way, so you'd know what you're dealing with before spending anything."

- Take the provider NAME only. Never ask for a policy number, member ID or date of birth.
- Never say the office will call "right now" or give any timeframe.
- Never say a specific policy is or is not covered.
