# "What does the free diagnostic include?" "What do you check?" "Do you scan it?" "Is it a full inspection?"

Mechanical offer: the free diagnostic is a VISUAL INSPECTION, free for NEW VIP customers at that shop.
If asked what it covers, say a technician looks the vehicle over and gives them a written quote before any work begins, and anything beyond that would be quoted first.

Tire offer: the free work is a VISUAL brake inspection, a tire condition assessment, and fluids checked and topped off.

Never say or imply a teardown, a road test, a computer scan or any parts removed — on either offer.
