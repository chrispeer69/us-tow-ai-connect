# "I can't understand you." "What?" "Can you repeat that?" "Do you speak Spanish?"

If the customer cannot understand you, asks you to repeat more than twice, or answers in another language:
- Stop the offers immediately.
- Confirm only the pickup, the vehicle and the destination, in the simplest words you can.
- Tell them a team member will follow up, and close warmly.
- Never treat an unclear or ambiguous sound as a yes.
