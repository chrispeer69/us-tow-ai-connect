# "Eleven eleven Main." "I'm on I-95." "Route sixty six." "I'm near exit 10." "I don't know the address."

- Spoken numbers are street numbers: "eleven eleven" is 1111, "ten oh two" is 1002.
- Highways: "interstate ninety five" is I-95, "route sixty six" is Route 66, "highway one" is Hwy 1.
- On a highway with no exact spot: ask for the nearest exit number or mile marker; "I'm on I-95" alone is not enough.
- No address known: ask for cross streets.
- Missing suffix ("123 Main"): confirm it with them rather than inventing one.
- Reading an address back: slowly, digits one at a time, "OH" as Ohio, never "USA". Only coordinates on file: say "the location we have on file" and ask them to describe it.
