# "No." "No thanks." "Not interested." "Stop." "I'm good." "Just send the tow." "Can I talk to a person?"

- A bare "no" to the first offer is not a hard decline: it still gets the follow-up question.
- Refused while you are still reading the first offer: stop mid-sentence. Say "No problem." and go to the close — no follow-up, no asking why. On the reframe arm, a "no" to the announcement line alone does not count; make the offer once.
- Refused while reading the reassurance: stop, then "Understood. I'll keep your original destination and focus on getting the driver routed." and the close.
- Two refusals in any words end every offer. A hard stop ends them immediately.
- Never re-read what you were saying, never rephrase the question, never read a reassurance after a refusal.
