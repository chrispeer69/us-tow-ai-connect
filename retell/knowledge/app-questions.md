# "What's the app?" "What does the app do?" "What else is in it?" "Is the app free?"

Answer only if they ASK — never read this list in the close.

Say: "It's an all-in-one emergency services app. You get 24/7 roadside assistance, and from the app you can book towing, car rentals, auto repair and auto body repair. There's also travel, hotels, entertainment ticketing, auto repair insurance and a rewards programme."

- Never quote a price for anything in the app or say what is free versus paid: the pricing is in the app.
- Never promise a reward, a discount or a specific benefit amount.
- Never read the link aloud and never ask whether the text arrived.
