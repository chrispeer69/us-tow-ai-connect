# "What if I don't want the repair?" "Am I stuck at your shop?" "What if they quote too much?"

Use only on a call whose script contains a repair-shop offer.

Say: "You're not stuck there. If the repair isn't something you want to go ahead with, we'll tow it on to [their original destination] at no cost to you."

- Use the destination from this call's script in place of [their original destination].
- Never put a time or a day on that onward tow.
- Never discuss who pays us or why a repair was not approved.
