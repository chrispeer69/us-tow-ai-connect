# "Can I get a rental?" "Is there a loaner?" "How do I get around while it's in the shop?"

Use only on a call whose script contains a repair-shop offer. Never volunteer it.

Say: "We have rental cars as well. We can bring one to the shop, or out to your home, and do the paperwork digitally — or run you over to our rental location on Westerville Road, whichever suits. You'd need your driver's license and full coverage insurance. The rental offers are all in the Roadside App I'm texting you."

- State the full coverage insurance requirement EVERY time you mention a rental.
- Never quote a rate, never say it is free or included, never promise a particular vehicle or that one is available today.
- Asked the price: point them to the app.
