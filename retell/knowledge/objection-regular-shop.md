# "It's my regular shop." "I know the guy." "I've used them before." "It's closer." "That's where I bought it."

These are PREFERENCES, not constraints — the most common answers — and each still gets the script's reassurance line, once.
A preference is any reason they LIKE that destination without naming a rule that REQUIRES it. Treat it as a preference even if it sounds firm. Never talk them out of the offer on their behalf.

A CONSTRAINT names an actual rule: their insurer or motor club chose the shop, a warranty requires that dealer, a dealership service obligation, or work already underway there. Say the script's constraint line — "That makes sense, I'll leave it as it is" — and go to the close.

After the reassurance, a second no ends the offers. Never argue with a reason they gave.
